# Antioxidant Screening Protocol v2.1 Addendum

Version 2.1; scope: Historical exploratory ranking policy. This addendum does not change the v2.0 candidate scope, ORAC-FL primary metric, H/L arm separation, Stage 1/Stage 2 framework, future formal Tier A standard, target hierarchy, or translational framework.

Historical ORAC-FL evidence has four uses. Tier A is high-confidence formally comparable evidence and remains the sole basis for a future formal numerical ranking; its existing requirements are unchanged. Tier B1 is exploratory comparable evidence: the analyte must be an unambiguous single compound; ORAC-FL, H/L arm, AAPH, fluorescein, Trolox, temperature, and the major buffer/solvent environment must be identifiable; the primary value must be traceable and reliably convertible to mol TE/mol; and there must be no known severe optical or aggregation artifact. Numerical purity, complete compound-specific linearity, complete optical QC, or a small number of reading parameters may be absent. Tier B2 is quantitative but low-comparability evidence: a numerical result and a credible compound identity exist, but deficient protocol metadata, normalization, variant compatibility, or interference control precludes exploratory pooling. Tier C is explicitly incompatible or non-convertible. Tier X lacks enough information for adjudication.

A Formal ORAC Ranking includes Tier A only. An Exploratory Literature-Derived ORAC Comparison may include Tier A and Tier B1 while retaining publication-level points, protocol differences, and evidence tiers. B2, C, and X do not enter the main numerical plot. Exploratory outputs must not be called a definitive ranking, validated Top20, or evidence of human antioxidant effects. H-ORAC and L-ORAC remain separate unless a validated bridge is available.

For at least two independent A/B1 records, report n, median, minimum, maximum, and IQR when n is at least four. One record is labelled a single-study exploratory estimate. Do not select the maximum, use an unstratified pooled mean, or count technical replicates as independent studies. Absence of eligible evidence is coded not_measured_or_not_usable, never zero.

A high plotted value means only that the compound had a high reported value in the included historical ORAC-FL literature. It does not establish human efficacy, clinical benefit, bioavailability, disease prevention, cellular redox regulation, or final experimental priority.
