# Study overview

Which defined food-derived compounds warrant testing in a controlled aqueous antioxidant assay, given that published activity depends on the method and conditions? The project proceeds from literature-based compound prioritization, to aqueous absorbance assay development, to candidate measurements, and then to prospective evaluation of the prioritization framework. This release covers the first step.

Food occurrence, chemical identity and exact assay material are distinct evidence questions. We preserve discrete chemical structures, including stereochemistry, and maintain reference standards as separate assay roles. The analysis estimates conditional literature activity and exposes uncertainty in source coverage. It provides a proposal for experimental qualification. See [Results 1](Results1.md).
