# Methods 1. Evidence-informed computational prioritization

We assembled chemical entities from the [source-linked discovery register](../../01_candidate_discovery/README.md) before activity-based prioritization. Cross-referenced chemical names, structures, molecular formulas, PubChem identifiers and food-occurrence evidence formed the candidate profiles. Exact salt, hydrate, stereochemistry, isomer or conjugation remained open where original reports could not resolve them. Pure materials supplied compound observations. Mixtures, extracts and calibration roles were retained as contextual evidence.

The primary window was 2020-01-01 through 2026-11-17 by first public release. The 2016-2019 search extension addressed specific evidence gaps. Earlier original experiments were retained as historical comparison strata. Searches used candidate names and aliases as well as method and compound-class routes across [available databases](../../02_identity_and_evidence/05_multidatabase_retrieval/Database_Coverage_Audit.md). DOI, PMID and other identifiers supported record deduplication. Automated hits supplied discovery leads. We selected original numerical reports for targeted assessment using the [capacity review](../../03_candidate_selection/08_capacity_review_validation/README.md). Review recorded the table or figure locator, compound material, method, pH, temperature, reported value and unit, error and replicate scope when available. Reference association and article identity alone did not establish independent experiments.

For the assay-specific synthesis, we ordered observed means only within a report, probe, protocol block, unit and direction. Higher capacity or relative response and lower scavenging concentration were favorable within their respective blocks. Equal values shared the earliest position. Censored or unqualified outcomes remained visible outside the ordered set. We retained original units and recognized molar TE equivalence only when the original denominator supported it. We avoided converting across distinct assays. Method sources include the [ORAC kinetic calibration](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox12020505), [2016 reference-relative assays](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules21020208), [FRAP and DPPH standard measurements](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules31172942), and [CUPRAC and ABTS standard measurements](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.talanta.2008.06.028).

The exploratory conditional ORAC-FL log-capacity model incorporated candidate and reported protocol-block effects, with fixed block shrinkage and whole-DOI holdout. We compared it with the training-candidate mean log-capacity baseline on the same estimable rows. The distinct structure Ridge audit used nested held-out scaffold groups within one source. The three prioritization dimensions were activity evidence, evidence reliability and prospective aqueous feasibility. Assay-specific activity and experimental priority were reported separately, with explicit manual rationales and no fixed composite weights. See the [analysis protocol](Model_and_Selection_Protocol.md).
