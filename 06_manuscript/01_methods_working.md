## Search, entity identification and counting

The primary publication window was January 1, 2020 through November 17, 2026. Searches used assay-method and compound-class routes, followed by candidate names and verified aliases when available. The [database audit](../02_identity_and_evidence/05_multidatabase_retrieval/Database_Coverage_Audit.md) supplies query scopes, run dates and route overlaps. PubMed returned 65,248 distinct PMIDs. Europe PMC returned 62,458 provisional route keys. The authenticated Web of Science Core Collection search reported 147,971 union hits, of which 500 were exported and imported. CNKI and Scopus contributed no screened export to this snapshot. PubChem supported formula, structure and alias reconciliation. The publication window is assessed against first public availability. Formal issue dates and indexing dates are separate. Earlier reports remain available in historical and gap-extension strata.

A compound-class discovery and identity workflow produced [204 register entries](../01_candidate_discovery/results/discovery_register.csv) and a [193-profile review universe](../02_identity_and_evidence/result/table/candidate_evidence_profiles.csv). The 193 profiles include unresolved identities, forms and eligibility decisions. The [new-entity queue](../02_identity_and_evidence/05_multidatabase_retrieval/Food_Compound_Discovery_Queue.csv) has 25,084 report leads. Assigning a new candidate requires a discrete, verifiable formula and structure and traceable dietary occurrence. Stereochemistry, positional isomer, glycoside, salt and hydrate distinctions are retained wherever distinguishable. Report keywords trigger review. Verified source and chemistry determine entity membership.

The screening code grouped accessible reports using DOI and PMID with existing reconciliation flags. Held identifiers and links carry forward to version and experiment audit. Searches, report keys, linked compounds and independent experiments have separate denominators. One report can contribute to several compounds. An author's two publications may cover the same experimental series. The [per-compound counts](../02_identity_and_evidence/result/table/01_candidate_report_coverage.csv) retain inherited links separately from the current machine-screen union and literal name hits. The [source-linked alias list](../02_identity_and_evidence/06_machine_screening/Search_Alias_Leads.csv) adds ascorbic acid and vitamin C as discovery terms. Each hit still requires exact-form confirmation. A zero name-match count is a discovery diagnostic. Short or otherwise unresolved name terms and spelling variants require recall checks.

## Machine routing and scientific eligibility

Titles and available abstracts were searched with versioned assay, canonical compound name and role rules. Roles are nonexclusive and record possible relevance. Retractions, notices and corrections are separately queued. Reviews can supply citations. Missing text and unmatched names retain pending status. The [QC counts](../02_identity_and_evidence/result/table/audit/Screening_QC.json) give denominators for full abstract and title-only processing, together with role and disposition counts. The DOI overlay and stratified review sample support recall auditing. For final methods reporting, report retrieval, deduplication, title and abstract assessment, full-text eligibility, and quantitative inclusion separately, with reasons and report IDs. See [PRISMA 2020](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n160).

Current screening counts, full-text batch progress and conditional model coverage are in the [Working Results](result/02_results_working.md). The [frozen reference](../02_identity_and_evidence/inputs/provenance/results1_v1.0/README.md) records the historical extraction and within-source ranking baseline.

| Scientific role | Eligibility for extraction or use |
|---|---|
| Quantitative antioxidant activity | Primary measurement, unambiguous compound form, original value and units, figure or table locator, named assay and endpoint, comparator and enough protocol and concentration information for the intended comparison. Preserve error type and independent n when reported. Unsupported quantities or incomparable endpoints remain descriptive. |
| Analytical detection | Original analytical method and identifiable analyte with material, sample and analytical selectivity evidence. Capture authentic-standard, MS/MS, spectral or other identity support as reported. |
| Dietary occurrence | Traceable food, edible portion or preparation with the actual chemical form and source locator. Ambiguous isomers or changes of form remain unresolved. |
| Conditions or modifying factors | Identify measured compound and outcome, context, comparison and variables such as processing, solvent, pH, storage or temperature. Distinguish altered sample content from altered measured antioxidant response. |

Confirmed retracted experiments are excluded from quantitative synthesis once the publisher or index provenance is verified. Reviews, repeated experiments and secondary values cannot contribute independent effects. A report can be irrelevant to activity modeling yet eligible for detection, food occurrence or modifying conditions. Missing text, unreconciled versions, unresolved material and ambiguous conversion are pending or restricted to the applicable role. Record the controlling reason for every role-specific decision. The final eligibility ledger should hold report key, DOI, compound and form, role, full-text and figure locator, exclusion reason, reviewer status and independent experiment group. ## Numeric analysis and decision rule

The conditional regression response is log molar Trolox-equivalent capacity. A numeric row enters a comparison only when the measured material, endpoint and in-well denominator are compatible. A mass-normalized molar TE capacity can be converted using the molecular weight of the assayed material. Apply the same conversion to its reported uncertainty. Preserve assay, final concentration, pH, temperature, vehicle, probe, net AUC calculation and reference calibration. Use the approved pH 7.4 and 37 C protocol strata as the target for direct primary comparison. Report other conditions and partially documented protocols in sensitivity strata. Existing connected model results pool conditionally through anchored protocol blocks and include historical and other strata.

The [current increment](../03_candidate_selection/10_orac_evidence_increment/README.md) refits the connected additive model y = mu + theta[compound] + b[study-protocol] + error. The model uses centered protocol blocks with ridge penalty 10. Its impact sensitivity starts with equal total DOI weight and attenuates typed high within-report CV. A DOI is a grouping proxy until version and experiment relations are checked. The underlying errors are not interchangeable: within-study SD or CV measures replicate variation, SE describes uncertainty in a mean, and adjusted between-report spread measures reproducibility of compound estimates under the available design.

A proposed next-stage precision weight is w = q / (v + tau^2) with caps on total influence per independent experiment family. Here q is a prespecified function of verified report fields, v is sampling variance on the log-capacity scale and tau is residual between-study heterogeneity after protocol adjustment. Comparable log sampling variance can be approximated by (SD / (mean * sqrt(n)))^2 for moderate CV and verified independent n. Missing SD or n is carried as missing, accompanied by equal-source and conservative variance scenarios. Estimate any heterogeneity benchmark using training data only. Shrink compound-specific dispersion toward the common estimate where few eligible independent sources exist. Compare percentile or median benchmarks before fixing a rule. Raw SDs in distinct units, impact factor and mention count do not supply model weights.

A candidate quality specification after the material, endpoint and denominator gates is q = (1 + k) / 6, where k counts five verified fields: reported in-well concentration and vehicle, specified pH and temperature, stated blank and calibration procedure, stated net AUC formula and duration, and explicitly independent replicates with typed uncertainty. Score each field only when both required parts are verified. The value is a reproducible reporting and comparability index. Test q against equal-weight fits and leave-report-out prediction before adopting it. During study-level validation, all parameter choices and reference dispersion must be estimated within the training split. The fitting code can score eligibility and weights automatically from the structured review ledger.

For each compound, the reference rank is its expanded conditional ORAC-FL rank. Combine the ranks from the available model scenarios with the conservative best and worst rank bounds from deleting each DOI in turn in the two-source sensitivity cohort. Retain a compound for further evaluation if both endpoints of the combined range are at most ten positions from its reference rank. A displacement of exactly ten positions passes. Hold a compound if it exceeds this limit or becomes unavailable in any deletion. The threshold applies across the full ranking and sets no cutoff by absolute rank. The [Figure 1 ranking table](../03_candidate_selection/result/table/04_figure1_candidate_ranks.csv) reports the endpoints, maximum displacement and decision. Experimental release requires separate material and assay qualification.

Previously explored support and dispersion terms are recorded in the [readiness audit](../03_candidate_selection/result/table/audit/Objective_Priority_Readiness.csv). The audit also records an earlier rank cutoff. Current decisions use the stability rule in Figure 1.

Other assays retain method-specific outcomes and direction. A cross-assay mapping requires matched pure materials and comparable endpoints under documented conditions and validation on held-out source and compound groups. The [FL/PGR overlap in the baseline analysis](../03_candidate_selection/09_aqueous_candidate_priority_v0.1/Ranking_Robustness_and_Uncertainty.md) reverses 52 of 78 matched compound pairs and provides no validated generic conversion. The structural Ridge model with molecular descriptors and fingerprints remains a sensitivity analysis under grouped scaffold validation, with single-source development scope in the audited run. Model predictions and compounds with qualifying original observations retain separate flags.
