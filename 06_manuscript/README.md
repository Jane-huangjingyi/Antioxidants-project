# 06. Manuscript assembly

Read [Methods](Methods.md), [generated descriptive Results](Results.md), [Figure captions](Figure_Captions.md) and [the claim map](tables/Manuscript_Claim_Map.csv). Each claim identifies its input/code/output chain and its scientific status.

Run `python run_pipeline.py` to regenerate current counts, Tables 1-3 and S1, Results and three main figures. The optional English workbook is a filtered review companion to those source tables. It is not the numerical authority.

Methods and Results currently cover source-bounded discovery, identity references, evidence organization, selection sensitivity and assay preparation. Experimental dose-response, cellular benefit and causal mechanism Results require real data and scientific review. Do not turn planned procedures into past-tense claims that an experiment was performed.
