# Top20 Evidence Consolidation Results

This round traced original literature for single-study Top20 entries and six high-importance missing candidates. Eighteen independent Tier B1 ORAC-FL records were added; Tier A remains zero. β-phycoerythrin ORAC, fluorescein-based HORAC, and RMCD-based L-ORAC records were explicitly excluded from the H-arm. Units, identities, significant digits, and conversions were rechecked. No numerical correction to an existing eligible record was required.

The updated top ten are epicatechin (9.670), catechin (9.000), quercetin (8.395), kaempferol (7.035), naringenin (6.820), rutin (6.705), genistein (6.625), quercitrin (6.470), caffeic acid (6.315), and trans-resveratrol (5.811 mol TE/mol). Quercetin fell from first to third after two lower independent values were added. Epicatechin and catechin moved to first and second. Quercitrin and caffeic acid each moved down four places mainly because new candidates entered above them, rather than because their own values were corrected.

Epicatechin increased from one to four B1 records, catechin from two to five, and quercetin from two to four; all three are now High confidence. Rutin, genistein, and p-coumaric acid increased from one to two and became Moderate. Quercitrin, trans-resveratrol, and gentisic acid remain single-study estimates. Caffeic acid remains supported by two records.

Kaempferol, naringenin, EGCG, and myricetin entered the Top20 at ranks 4, 5, 12, and 14. Gallic acid, tyrosol, carvacrol, and thymol exited. Two eligible EGCG B1 records remained after exclusion of β-phycoerythrin, HORAC, L-ORAC, and non-extractable figure-only evidence. Thus, its previous absence reflected evidence tracing and assay classification, not demonstrated weak antioxidant capacity.

Cyanidin-3-O-glucoside has an exact historical ORAC-FL value but remains B2 because compound-specific controls for spectral overlap, inner-filter effects, self-fluorescence, quenching, and pH transformation were insufficiently documented. No usable parent-secoisolariciresinol ORAC-FL record was found; mammalian enterolignans were not substituted for the dietary parent. Anthocyanins and lignans therefore remain systematically under-covered.

Eleven of the updated Top20 meet the Ready for target mapping rule; nine are Conditionally ready because they retain single-study evidence. This supports freezing `Target_Mapping_Shortlist_v0.1` as a tiered next-stage list. The result remains literature-derived and provisional: all eligible evidence is B1, protocol heterogeneity remains, and publication coverage is concentrated in flavan-3-ols and flavonols.
