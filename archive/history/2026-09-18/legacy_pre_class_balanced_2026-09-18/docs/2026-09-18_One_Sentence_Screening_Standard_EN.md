The project defines primary chemical antioxidant capacity as the kinetically integrated chain-breaking ability of a structurally defined compound to inhibit AAPH-derived peroxyl-radical oxidation of fluorescein under one locked ORAC-FL protocol, expressed as `mol Trolox equivalents/mol compound`. This metric ranks chemical capacity only, does not imply antioxidant effects in humans, cannot directly pool values from different protocols, and does not exclude candidates with missing ORAC data.
