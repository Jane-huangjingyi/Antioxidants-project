# Top20 Evidence Change Log

Eighteen independent B1 records were added: Tabart 2009 for EGCG, kaempferol, myricetin, naringenin, epicatechin, catechin, and quercetin; Zhang 2013 for EGCG, kaempferol, naringenin, epicatechin, catechin, quercetin, and genistein; and Rothe 2023 for epicatechin, catechin, rutin, and p-coumaric acid. An exact cyanidin-3-O-glucoside value was retained as B2 because anthocyanin-specific optical interference controls were insufficient.

EGCG, kaempferol, naringenin, and myricetin entered the Top20; gallic acid, tyrosol, carvacrol, and thymol exited. Epicatechin, catechin, and quercetin became High confidence. Rutin, genistein, and p-coumaric acid became Moderate. No second eligible B1 was found for quercitrin, trans-resveratrol, or gentisic acid.

No previous conversion error was found. Compound identity, glycoside/aglycone status, molecular-weight basis, units, and significant digits were rechecked. Values from Ou 2002 (DOI 10.1021/jf011480w) were classified as HORAC and excluded. RMCD L-ORAC records and β-phycoerythrin or non-extractable figure-only evidence remain outside the H-arm ranking.
