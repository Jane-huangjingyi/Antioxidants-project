# Experimental validation plan

Begin with six tier-A compounds for a qualification panel. Treat catechin and epicatechin as a matched stereochemical pair. Gallic acid and 5-caffeoylquinic acid cover two phenolic chemistries. L-ascorbic acid and reduced GSH span nonphenolic redox mechanisms. Quercetin can serve as a separate solubility and optical challenge after its material gates clear. Procurement, lot, purity, final-well solubility and stability remain pending for all candidates. Keep Trolox in the calibration/control role.

Use Crocin bleaching as the draft water-phase radical-protection method on the Opentrons Flex 450 nm channel, subject to probe, temperature, dead-time and mix/read qualification. CUPRAC at 450 nm is a distinct reduction-capacity option. Published PGR at 540 nm and common ABTS at 734 nm require a validated optical setup. The 2016 ABTS 651 nm protocol uses a distinct radical generation system and needs an independent 650 nm feasibility check. See [the method design](../../04_05_experimental_validation/01_endpoint_definition/README.md) and [platform compatibility](../../04_05_experimental_validation/03_platform_compatibility/README.md).

Record exact reagent structure, stereochemistry, salt/hydrate and molecular weight, catalogue, lot, COA and purity. Define pH, temperature and elapsed times, confirm final dissolved concentration and stability at the intended vehicle fraction, and report the exact scale. Choose a preapproved concentration ladder from the soluble, linear range. Record batch and dispense timing, randomize well positions, balance order and edge positions, and use independent preparations and days. Technical wells are nested within preparations.

Include matched vehicle, reagent blank, no-initiator, compound-only/no-probe, compound plus probe without initiator, probe plus initiator, and Trolox calibration wells. Inspect color, scattering, precipitation, baseline drift, probe-only chemistry and direct effects on radical generation. Compare drift and optical effects to matched blanks and check absence of carryover. Mark concentration points invalid if preapproved interference or stability criteria fail, then retain raw traces and reasons.

For a verified shared linear region, estimate molar TE from the slope ratio of matched net protective AUC versus molar concentration. Report integration window and reference fit, and report rate and lag separately. Reject flat calibration and confirm window sensitivity and repeatability. Predefine the effect and interference thresholds after a pilot, before the ranking run. If a simple linear operational capacity is unsuitable, use a qualified fixed-response endpoint and report its definition. ORAC-FL evidence remains unpaired contextual comparison. No in vivo claim, laboratory run or scientific release is made here.
