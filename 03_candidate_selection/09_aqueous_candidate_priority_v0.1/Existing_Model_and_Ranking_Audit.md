# Existing model and ranking audit

This release is a Stage 2 targeted prioritization supplement. The primary literature window is 2020-01-01 to 2026-11-17 by first public release. The 2016-2019 expansion addresses specific assay gaps. Earlier original studies appear as dated historical comparators. Food identity and occurrence references can predate the capacity review window. The discovery framework retains 193 profiles, including exploratory profiles. Defined food compounds are the candidate target, with Trolox and other standards managed separately. Class-level name or food matches remain provisional.

Step 03.8 audits 203 numeric source rows from 15 DOIs, including holds and distinct endpoints. In this release, 51 compounds have at least one conditional ORAC-FL within-source comparison. Their study, solvent, probe, dose, material form and original units are shown in the assay workbook. Some older entries are normalized Trolox ratios or parent-mass assumptions. A result at one dose or from one DOI supports that particular observation. There is no common quantitative water-phase absorbance target, independent material variance or fully comparable study network to justify a new global model.

Source check paths are the generated summaries under [activity model](../01_activity_model/README.md), [first fit](../04_first_evidence_model/README.md), [robustness](../05_model_robustness/README.md), [verified update](../06_verified_source_update/README.md), [all-reference integration](../07_all_reference_integration/README.md), and [capacity review](../08_capacity_review_validation/README.md). The all-reference register remains bibliographic context.

The discovery base contains 65,248 PubMed PMIDs, 62,458 Europe PMC broad-route report keys and 500 reconciled local WoS export rows from 147,971 WoS hits. A local DOI join matched four of the 18 original-report DOIs used in this overlay to the first WoS export. The remaining 147,471 WoS hits are searched and not exported. Search overlap and identity reconciliation prevent adding these numbers to count independent studies. The WoS remainder is paused. Bibliographic-only records supplied no potency values. The overlay did not perform full-corpus screening or assign new food identities from the discovery queue.
