# Targeted literature gap review

This release reused the Stage 03.8 audit and selected original comparison panels. Rechecked source endpoints include [Zhang 2020 methods and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/4150897), [2023 ORAC kinetic calibration, Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox12020505), [2026 FRAP and DPPH Table 1](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules31172942), [2016 comparison Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules21020208) and [2008 normal CUPRAC and ABTS Table 3](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.talanta.2008.06.028). Extracted numerical subsets retain table/column references. The 2016 comparison is a gap-specific extension. The 2008 panel and older FL/PGR rows are historical comparators. Trolox calibration and DPPH censored rows in Zhang 2020 deserve a separate structured supplement extraction before inclusion.

1. Compare rosmarinic acid and gallocatechin in at least one additional original FL study with eligible material and documented well concentration, pH, vehicle and comparator. In 10.3390/antiox11091784 verify reported versus final dose.
2. For ascorbate and reduced GSH, seek a second condition-matched independent source or matched bench data. Keep 2023 Table 3 TE-AUC separate from its lag-time estimates and retain solvent blocks.
3. Resolve the denominator conflict for trans-tiliroside and (+)-pinoresinol (DOI 10.1080/14786419.2025.2493187). Prior supplement review located Figure S5, with a mol denominator on the axis and a micromol denominator in Methods 3.4. Preserve the hold pending the exact means, SEM, ORAC-specific n, and material disposition. Investigate the carotenoid paper 10.1016/j.foodchem.2025.146338 separately for aqueous applicability.
4. For sesamin check 10.1007/s13197-014-1334-6 Figure 2 and the dose denominator and initiator conflict. The isolated 10.1371/journal.pone.0251534 value has no shared compound in that component. Retain both distinction and scope.
5. For Crocin, seek a usable same-protocol panel spanning ascorbate, a flavanol and a phenolic acid, and inspect [the purified crocin methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3586850/). Method and extract papers do not supply a comparable candidate rank here.
6. Prioritize species- and form-specific sources for quercetin, hesperetin, caffeic acid and protocatechuic acid. Confirm edible material, free versus conjugated forms, positional identity and relevant analyte standards. Food discovery leads stay provisional until those checks resolve.

Expand only where a new independent source, a shared comparator, a quantitative endpoint or a resolved material/protocol gap can change the decision. Pause after those fields are resolved or after targeted source access is exhausted and the hold is documented. No whole-WoS manual export is required for this release.
