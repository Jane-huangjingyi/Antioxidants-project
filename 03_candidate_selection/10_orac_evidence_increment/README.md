# ORAC-FL evidence increment and dispersion sensitivity

Run `python 03_candidate_selection/code/01_fit_orac_fl.py` from the project root. The increment operates on the preserved Results 1 model inputs and writes separate outputs. All source decisions are pending scientific review.

[Source review](../inputs/provenance/10_orac_evidence_increment/Source_Review.csv) covers six primary reports. [Original extraction](../inputs/provenance/10_orac_evidence_increment/Extracted_Observations.csv) retains nine pure-compound observations. Takatsuka 2021 and the 2025 assay comparison add six observations and two DOI reports to the expanded conditional fit. All five affected identities were already connected. The 2021 authors give molar Trolox-normalized FL results and report triplicate measurement. Table 1 uncertainty rows apply to DPPH and ABTS. ORAC uncertainty is absent. The 2025 table gives molar TE under a separately coded normalized fluorescence-protection protocol. Sections 3.11-3.12 confirm molar TE and mean +/- SD from at least three experiments. Calibration construction and independence are unresolved. The sensitivity analysis without this DOI tests that qualification. These source comparisons remain conditional.

[Harmonization](../result/table/audit/Harmonized_New_Observations.csv) retains raw and standardized quantities with source locators. A micromole-per-micromole ratio has the same magnitude as mol TE/mol. Multipliers for micromol TE/g and mmol TE/g are material MW multiplied by 1e-6 and 1e-3, respectively. The same multiplier applies to mean and dispersion. A solution-level TE value needs the matching in-well analyte concentration and a validated common endpoint before it can be interpreted as molar capacity. Neither unmatched volume values nor mixture, lag-time or different-probe results enter this fit. The 2024 quercetin entry converts conditionally from 22.23 +/- 3.19 mmol TE/g to 6.7188 +/- 0.9641 mol TE/mol on the anhydrous parent basis. Its exact powder form requires confirmation. It enters only the form sensitivity scenario. Two 2026 standards remain on hold for calibration and solvent questions, including reported high-dose nonlinearity for quercetin. The red cabbage source additionally contains single-standard slopes in Figure 2; check axis scales, matching Trolox calibration and overlap with the 2025 author group before using these as an independent source.

[Model input](../result/table/01_connected_model_input.csv) and [row weights](../result/table/audit/Observation_Weights.csv) retain model values, reported error designation and evidence IDs. The original centered protocol-block model with penalty 10 is reused. Equal-total-DOI weights use 1 divided by the number of rows in that report. For a typed SD the sensitivity multiplies that starting weight by max(0.10, 1 / (1 + (CV / 0.25)^2)), where CV is SD divided by the comparable mean. Ratios of 25 and 50 percent give multipliers of 0.50 and 0.20. We used thresholds 0.15 and 0.40 as alternate scales. The multiplier changes influence but leaves the reported mean intact. Unknown error, untyped plus/minus, or missing SD receives the unmodified DOI-balanced factor and a visible flag. Independent n is not inferred from wells or from the number of measurements. This is a descriptive reliability sensitivity, without inverse-variance pooling or formal sampling intervals. No typed row in this increment's connected dataset reaches 25 percent; maximum was 11.90 percent, and 63 of 119 rows lack documented SD. The model never penalizes measurement spread quoted in an incomparable absolute unit.

[Ranking and variability](../result/table/audit/Ranking_and_Variability.csv) separates within-report CV from the variation in adjusted log means across DOI reports. The latter uses one median adjusted value per DOI and reports a descriptive SD for candidates with at least three DOI reports. It is not an independent sampling variance or a pure between-study variance component. A large spread calls for checking solvent, material and endpoint and comparing leave-one-DOI-out ranks. No automatic penalty is assessed on a compound for real effects across protocols.

[Increment summary](../result/model/01_orac_fl_fit_summary.json) reports scoreable whole-report validation denominators and matched baselines. The expanded result scores a different set of rows than the prior model. The weighted provisional screen retains quercetin, catechin and hesperetin under the two-report, top-20 and conservative deletion rule. Curated Results 1 tiers and study approval records are preserved.
