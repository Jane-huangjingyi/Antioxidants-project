# Cross-study and cross-structure measurement bridge

The twelve-profile panel in `results/Bridge_Panel_Preparation.csv` is a method-development proposal. It covers shared-source compounds and chemical/material contrasts needed to test transportability; it is not the experimental Top20 or a potency ranking. Its explicit selection rationale is in `inputs/bridge_panel.json`. Ascorbate and glutathione provide nonphenolic chemistry and source overlap, without receiving a classic-reference exemption or an efficacy bonus. Source-reference peptides outside the current candidate index do not automatically enter this panel or the candidate universe.

First verify candidate-specific dietary evidence, exact molecular form, stereochemistry, hydrate/salt basis, purity and lot. Prepare molar stocks using the actual material basis. A parent reference formula is not a reagent certificate. Resolve solubility, stability and interference before interpreting capacity. Gallic-acid solvent uncertainty and catechin/epicatechin form matching are explicit bridge questions, not silently corrected source errors.

On 2026-11-09 the researcher confirmed that only non-fluorescent measurements can currently be arranged. The active route is therefore a standalone absorbance-capacity model, not calibration from ORAC-FL. Literature ORAC-FL at pH 7.4 and 37 C remains a protocol-stratified prior/source-model reference; it is not the target assay's measured label. No fluorescence arm or numerical transfer validation is assumed.

Start endpoint development from the existing Step 04 crocin-capacity plan. ABTS and PGR retain their distinct scientific roles but their audited wavelengths are not directly supported by the identified standard reader configuration. These remain method-development candidates, not selected/approved protocols. Define and validate an assay-specific molar-capacity response first, then collect real labels and evaluate the standalone model. Do not copy the literature model's pH, temperature, integration window or capacity units into an absorbance protocol without validation. Directly rank only comparable measured/validated responses under the declared target conditions; numerical ORAC-FL equivalence remains unsupported.

Range finding determines soluble, interference-cleared concentration series and usable kinetic windows. The reader model is identified in the [platform qualification](../../04_absorbance_assay/03_platform_compatibility/README.md); exact labware, concentrations, final solvent fractions, replicate counts and acceptance thresholds remain unset. Actual accessory inventory and thermal/timing qualification are pending. Trolox calibration and blanks are analytical controls, not extra candidate-panel members. Match sample/calibrator vehicles within each method. Include reagent/vehicle blanks, sample-only optical controls, no-radical controls where appropriate, and independent preparations rather than treating technical wells as independent experiments. Randomize compound/dose positions within batch and record addition/read timing and plate position. Retain zeros, non-detections, failed interference screens and invalid measurements as distinct outcomes.

Before definitive runs, lock endpoint integration, calibration/valid range, exclusions, repeat rules and the model-validation plan using pilot precision and assay objectives. Record negative results. Preserve raw time courses and all metadata using the existing Step 04 schema and analysis code; do not populate it with invented experimental rows. Use batch-grouped validation, plus compound/class holdouts for transportability, with preprocessing and model selection inside training folds. Compare against simple baselines and report error, calibration and order stability. Confidence/interval claims require a justified measurement and validation design; five refit ranges are not confidence intervals.

The current source-specific model remains an exploratory baseline. Formal global screening begins only after compatible, source-verified labels cover the intended chemical domain and show validated predictive value. No fixed sample-size sufficiency or passing error threshold is inferred from this proposed twelve-member panel. Procurement, actual experiment execution and final selection are not performed by this plan.
