This conclusion is conditional on the fixed candidate set and the fitted source-block model. Sampling uncertainty, compound-specific protocol effects and unseen candidates require additional evidence.

Source-deletion bounds include non-estimable comparisons. The source DOI is the independence unit. Technical replicates and multiple rows within a paper do not add independent studies. Empty errors and missing pH are reported explicitly. No IF or sampling-precision score is assigned.
