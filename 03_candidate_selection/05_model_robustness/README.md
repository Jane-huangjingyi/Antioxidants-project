# Second-stage model robustness

Run `python 03_candidate_selection/05_model_robustness/compare_models.py` from the project root. This module reuses the first-stage 34-candidate, 11-paper connected component and its unresolved cross-protocol reference inputs. It compares candidate-mean baseline, additive OLS and Huber regression on the same 35 estimable leave-paper-out observations. New-paper prediction assumes the training-centered average paper effect.

Huber iteratively reweights residuals using a 1.345 cutoff and MAD scale, with a maximum of 100 iterations and coefficient tolerance 1e-8. This residual-based weighting addresses sensitivity to extreme residuals. Article methods, source validity and experimental quality still require their own assessments. Huber MAE/RMSE are approximately 0.326/0.415 log units, compared with OLS 0.318/0.408 and baseline 0.326/0.391. These results support retaining the baseline as a benchmark while further data qualification proceeds. Model selection from these previously inspected data remains exploratory.

We also perturb each paper's total influence using 300 independent sets of positive exponential weights, shared by all records from the same DOI, with seed 20261111. Each perturbed fit retains the observed candidate-paper graph and sum-to-zero paper-effect convention. Quantiles describe sensitivity of conditional estimates and positions to those weights. They carry no sampling-confidence or posterior-probability interpretation. Missing studies, unobserved bias, uncertain original values, material differences and disconnected components are outside this perturbation. Single-source candidates remain conditional on their source.

`results/Candidate_Model_Sensitivity.csv` reports OLS and Huber component positions and paper-weight sensitivity quantiles. `results/Heldout_Model_Comparison.csv` retains matched predictions and observed values. `results/robustness_summary.json` records metrics, seed and input/code hashes. The mathematical target is a log location with additive paper effects. An arithmetic mean, quality-adjusted capacity and validated Top20 probability require additional modelling assumptions and data.

## Relative-order estimability

Each leave-paper-out fit also evaluates candidate-pair contrast vectors (+1 for one candidate and -1 for the other) against the training design row space. Pairwise differences can be estimable when absolute locations are unidentified. `Pairwise_Source_Omission.csv` retains each contrast and estimability flag. `Pairwise_Order_Summary.csv` records ranges and pairs whose direction agrees in the full fit and every omission, with contrast estimability in all omissions. These deterministic direction checks use numerical tolerance 1e-8. They carry no significance test or confidence level.

`Candidate_Partial_Order.csv` counts direct stable higher/lower relationships and gives possible-position bounds in the 34-candidate component. At least h stable higher candidates imply position >= h+1; l stable lower candidates imply position <= 34-l. Unresolved comparisons widen the bounds. These are partial-order constraints under the model. Data-quality, protocol and measurement-bias issues remain outside this algebraic check.

Compare arithmetic source means, geometric source means, exponentiated OLS log locations and exponentiated OLS multiplied by the mean exponentiated training residual. ## Penalized paper-effect candidates

Following inspection of earlier validation, we added two exploratory paper-effect shrinkage strengths, lambda = 1 and 10. The objective is residual sum of squares plus lambda times the sum of squared paper effects, with sum-to-zero paper effects and unpenalized candidate locations. The contrast parameterization includes the last paper effect in the penalty, preserving the symmetric penalty across papers. Each held-out fit uses training outcomes only. Candidate predictions retain the same 35 estimable observations for comparison.

Shrinkage improves several exploratory diagnostics. Lambda 10 gives held-out log MAE/RMSE approximately 0.318/0.385 and within-paper direction agreement 0.783, compared with baseline 0.326/0.391 and 0.750. Lambda 1 gives MAE/RMSE 0.312/0.387 and direction agreement 0.767. Relative log-capacity ratio errors are also recorded. Candidate full-fit positions for both strengths are added to Candidate_Model_Sensitivity.csv. Source-omission constraints and weight-perturbation ranges in that table still refer to the existing unpenalized model. Hyperparameter development is post-hoc on inspected outcomes. A chosen shrinkage strength requires nested selection or new validation before confirmatory performance claims.
