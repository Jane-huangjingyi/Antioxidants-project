## Connected phenolic-acid trial

[Wang et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12523838/), sections 2.1, 2.2.4, 2.11 and 3.1, reports four individually assayed phenolic acid standards in Table 1. The protocol specifies 75 mM buffer at pH 7.4, a 37 °C preincubation, fluorescein, AAPH, Trolox, a PBS blank and a 100-minute net-AUC read. Section 2.11 specifies SEM over three to seven independent experiments across the paper. The exact ORAC row-level count was not assigned. Table 1 specifies mmol TE/mmol, numerically identical to mol TE/mol. The prose in section 3.1 labels the gallic-acid result mM, leaving a reporting inconsistency. The four [original values](../../02_identity_and_evidence/inputs/provenance/ORAC_Four_Phenolic_Acids_2025.csv) were therefore used under an explicit Table 1 unit assumption in the trial only. SEM was preserved and never treated as SD by the model weights. The study is outside the 567-report priority queue and was absent from the first-round fit.

Run `python 03_candidate_selection/code/07_trial_phenolic_acids.py` in the NumPy-enabled environment to generate the [51-compound comparison](Trial_Phenolic_Acid_Rank_Comparison.csv) and [holdout diagnostics](Trial_Phenolic_Acid_Diagnostics.json). The trial used the same conditional model with one added study block, for 129 observations from 15 DOI reports and the same 51 compounds. The matched 105-row whole-DOI holdout log-RMSE was 0.735 for the first round and 0.728 for the trial. The trial stability range uses the CV-weighted fit, the DOI-balanced fit, and conservative whole-DOI deletion bounds on the trial cohort. The first-round status uses the original manuscript's wider set of previously reported protocol scenarios. Compare point ranks on the shared universe and interpret status changes with this difference in scenario scope.

Protocatechuic acid moved from 6 to 24 and p-coumaric acid moved from 36 to 26 in the trial. Ferulic acid moved from 42 to 40 and gallic acid from 48 to 47. The main analysis and the formal updated-rank fields remain at their previous states while the table/prose units are clarified. Additional original data access was pursued for [Tavadyan and Minasyan 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12039-019-1618-5), a potential shared source for morin, quercetin, rutin, naringin and ascorbic acid. The publisher landing page alone did not establish extractable single-compound methods and values.
