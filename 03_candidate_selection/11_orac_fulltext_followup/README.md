# Targeted ORAC full-text follow-up

This follow-up uses the prior connected ORAC-FL model as its baseline. It records scoped source review for seven DOI reports and tests the effect of the recovered quantitative data. Scientific decisions remain pending. Prior Results 1 and the previous model inputs are preserved.

## Source screening and extraction

The 567 machine-prioritized records were reconciled against earlier source scopes and Results 1. Sixty-nine have reusable recorded scopes, five received a scoped review in this batch, 26 already have access or scope work queued and 467 await a new scope. Counts are provisional report records. The local queue is at `download_raw/stage2_machine_screening/ORAC_Fulltext_Review_Queue.csv`. Two additional reports with useful evidence came from the low-priority queue. This illustrates the value of citation tracing and recall review before any automated exclusions.

[Source decisions](../inputs/provenance/11_orac_fulltext_followup/Source_Review.csv) link each report to its original location and review scope. [Original observations](../inputs/provenance/11_orac_fulltext_followup/Original_Observations.csv) and [harmonized observations](../result/table/02_harmonized_observations.csv) record 14 pure-standard ORAC-FL values from two reports, including three with unresolved exact identities. Independent replicate count remains blank for both reports. Reported n values describe the authors' repeats. The first report tested standards alongside humic materials. Only its separate pure-standard table was used. The second reports a molar response in Table 1 and an ORAC method at pH 7.0, with unresolved reaction temperature and Trolox calculation wording. Its data are used only as a protocol sensitivity scenario. Secondary assays and nonmolar AUC indices retain their original evidence roles in the source ledger.

For conversion from micromoles TE per milligram, multiply the mean and reported SD by reference molecular weight in grams per mole and by 0.001. The calculation assumes the free compound represented by the named parent. Ascorbic acid 2.32 +/- 0.21 micromol TE/mg becomes 0.4086 +/- 0.0370 mol TE/mol on the L-ascorbic acid parent basis. The observed ORAC protocol reports pH 7.4, 37 C and a blank-adjusted AUC. The original report does not provide the same detail on pure-standard dosing or preparation as for its main materials. Six provisionally matched standards enter the conditional fit. Vitamin E, hydroxybenzoic acid and coumaric acid stay on identity hold. Rosmarinic acid 5.16 +/- 0.13 mol TE/mol is already on the target unit scale. Its ORAC condition keeps it in protocol sensitivity only.

## Ranking diagnostics

[Rank sensitivity](../result/table/03_rank_sensitivity.csv) gives 51 compound placements and whole-DOI deletion bounds. The prior fit uses 13 DOI reports and 119 observations. One conditional addition gives 14 DOI reports and 125 observations. Adding both reports gives 15 DOI reports and 130 observations in the sensitivity cohort. These counts are report proxies for source support. They do not certify the number of independent experimental preparations. No new model coefficient or candidate is promoted to the frozen analysis.

The centered protocol-block regression uses the previous ridge penalty and DOI-balanced CV weighting. A stricter descriptive weighting scenario uses the 75th percentile of the observed typed relative SDs, approximately 0.060, in place of the existing 0.25 reference. It measures sensitivity to the weight scale. The rank table reports the across-DOI SD of block-adjusted log means separately. The two SD measures have different interpretations. Independent n and comparable sampling variances remain incomplete, so the descriptive CV scenario does not supply inverse-variance weights or confidence intervals.

[QC and model summary](../result/model/02_whole_doi_validation.json) provides whole-DOI validation including a common test set of 81 scoreable prior rows. On those rows, log-scale RMSE changes from 0.595 to 0.518 with the conditional report and to 0.521 with both reports. Expanded-cohort metrics also score newly estimable records and therefore have different denominators. The two-report sensitivity places rosmarinic acid fifth and its worst DOI deletion bound is 23. Ascorbic acid remains 50th with seven DOI reports in the conditional and expanded analyses. Top20 membership varies by source and CV scale. Numerical ranks are exploratory.

## Reproduce

From the repository root run `python 03_candidate_selection/code/02_validate_and_rank.py`. Run the [screening pipeline](../../02_identity_and_evidence/06_machine_screening/README.md) first if its local outputs are absent. QC checks input hashes, duplicate evidence IDs, source overlap, minimum two shared compound anchors per added report, and the original priority denominator. Report-level intake remains local. Reconcile pure-standard calibration and form, continue the unscreened ORAC work queue and audit lower-priority retrievals before testing the proposed precision-weighted selection rule.
