# Food occurrence and source material follow-up

Run `build_food_form_followup.py` for `results/Food_and_Material_Followup.csv`. All 17 candidates have an explicit disposition, DOI and access scope. Five caffeoylquinic-acid candidates have original coffee-table support (Figure 1 and Table 2, DOI 10.1016/j.jare.2019.01.002). Positional nomenclature must still be cross-checked. Seven other candidates have primary-abstract occurrence support, not full-text closure. Four honey phenolic-acid leads remain pending full-source checking; vanillic-acid co-elution prevents treating an indexed peak label as unequivocal identification.

Eriodictyol requires separate treatment: DOI 10.3177/jnsv.52.54 describes enzyme-generated aglycones from lemon glycosides. It supports a food-derived preparation, not natural free-aglycone occurrence. No automatic scope inclusion or exclusion is made. The sesamol evidence also preserves source dependence: a 1982 survey reported non-detection whereas a later method was applied to market oils. Processing, analytical limits and sample composition must be checked before generalization.

Original antioxidant Methods for DOI 10.3390/foods10081807 report eugenol CAS 97-53-0 at purity at least 99%, capsaicin CAS 404-86-4 at least 95%, and vanillin CAS 121-33-5 at least 97%. These are source-reported reagent properties, not analytical certificates for a future procurement lot. Supplier catalog number, salt/hydrate state where relevant, batch, stability and matched material documentation remain separate gates. Five isolated caffeoylquinic compounds were structurally analyzed in their antioxidant source using spectroscopic methods, but individual positional assignment and purity records have not been fully inspected here.

The registry records food occurrence and material evidence separately from antioxidant potency. None is a score increment or a human approval. Original profiles remain unchanged, all human_review_status values are NOT_REVIEWED, and change_applied is NO. Follow-up fields now join the 99-row analysis entry by candidate ID without replacing historical source fields. Source CAS/purity properties always carry their own DOI and must not be transferred to a different paper or purchased batch. The main workbook now includes the Food and materials tab with these scoped fields and additional source checks.

Further scoped checks are in `inputs/additional_food_source_checks.json`. The honey publisher-indexed method passage describes alkaline hydrolysis, so the observed fraction cannot be described as native free acid without further verification. Direct full-text access still returned 403. DOI 10.1248/yakushi1947.103.9_997 reports eriodictyol isolated from peanut pericarps in its original abstract. This supports botanical occurrence but does not establish routine edible-part exposure; the candidate's food gate stays pending. Neither hydrolysis nor a plant's edible reputation substitutes for matching compound, chemical form and sampled tissue.
