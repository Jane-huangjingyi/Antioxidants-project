# Primary Antioxidant Metric Recommendation

Conditionally adopt ORAC-FL as the single primary chemical antioxidant-capacity metric. Its exact meaning is the competitive, kinetically integrated chain-breaking capacity of a defined compound to inhibit AAPH-derived peroxyl-radical oxidation of fluorescein under a locked protocol, reported for pure compounds as `mol Trolox equivalents/mol compound`. It describes one chemical reaction system and does not measure antioxidant effects in humans, health benefit, absorption, bioavailability, or cellular redox regulation.

ORAC-FL is selected because peroxyl-radical chain-breaking is more relevant to propagation chemistry than decolorizing a persistent artificial radical or simply reducing a metal; the full kinetic curve and net AUC integrate magnitude and duration; Trolox calibration supports molar normalization; and H-ORAC plus an interlaboratory-validated L-ORAC route can accommodate hydrophilic and lipophilic compounds. Publication volume is not the justification.

The recommendation is restrictive. ORAC values from different papers must not be directly pooled, and H-ORAC and L-ORAC are not automatically one numerical scale. The main ranking should preferably come from one laboratory using one locked SOP. External values become poolable only when compound form/purity, AAPH/fluorescein/Trolox, buffer/pH, temperature, solvent/RMCD, concentration linearity, read schedule, AUC calculation, and QC are equivalent and common bridging standards demonstrate acceptable laboratory bias.

DPPH is not primary because its nonphysiological persistent radical, steric accessibility, solvent, and time can materially change rank. ABTS•+ is also artificial and varies with generation, pH, solvent, and endpoint. FRAP measures acidic ferric reducing power rather than chain-breaking. CUPRAC is a useful near-neutral ET complement but remains reducing power. TRAP has strong mechanistic relevance but heterogeneous detection and calculation prevent a scalable primary ranking.

The formal framework is ORAC-FL as primary; CUPRAC, ABTS, DPPH, FRAP, TRAP, and a prespecified lipid-peroxidation model as secondary; and HORAC, TOSC, superoxide/hydroxyl/metal-chelation assays as supportive. Do not apply a default z-score composite or simple average. Code compounds without ORAC as `not_measured`, retain them, and prioritize standardized measurement; do not assign zero or exclude them. If approved, the next step is to freeze the ORAC-FL SOP and QC criteria before collecting harmonized experimental data, rather than immediately pooling heterogeneous literature values.
