The candidate scope is restricted to structurally defined single small molecules that occur naturally in normal diets and map uniquely to a PubChem CID/InChIKey, covering phenolic/polyphenolic subclasses, vitamin C, tocopherols/tocotrienols, carotenoids, organosulfur compounds, and a limited set of other clearly food-derived classes. Candidates enter H-ORAC or L-ORAC according to physicochemical properties, with special-case flags for solubility, aggregation, optical interference, instability, or noncanonical redox mechanisms; H/L arms are not pooled without bridging validation.
