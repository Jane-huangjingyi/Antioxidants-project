# Research decisions and open gates

The scope is individually defined food or dietary chemicals. Discovery precedes potency screening. The reconstructed 191-name enumeration is a historical seed collection, with documented additions and retained exclusions. It is not an exhaustive food chemical database.

Candidate-specific food occurrence, exact test form, procurement, solution stability and absorbance counter-screens require documented review. The researcher records initial screening in `03_candidate_selection/config/researcher_decisions.csv`. Blank decisions stay unknown. Generated Excel decisions are a snapshot, not a second input authority.

Twenty review slots are a preparation budget. Candidate IDs settle display ties only. All 12 candidates in the cutoff stratum remain visible for scientific resolution. No formal efficacy weights, journal scores, experimental Top20 or automatic scientific approval are introduced.

ORAC-PGR is the leading absorbance method to validate on the available equipment. The plate reader, dispensing limits, stock and final concentrations, reaction timing and acceptance thresholds remain unapproved. The published 800 mM AAPH statement requires source clarification before any protocol adoption. ABTS and crocin measurements retain their own constructs and units.

Original observations, historical source HOLDs, governance approvals and frozen H-ORAC assets retain their recorded status. A current branch or a regenerated figure does not supersede those scientific decisions. The new figures describe discovery and preparation; no new assay experiment has been performed.
